Features

You can sell, invest, buy creatures. Creatures get valued based on how many battles they won, the higher the value of the creature they fight, the higher the reward when winning.

You have a Brokerdex with the creatures you invested in

Each creature has their unique history in the simulated world

  • Creatures may birth new creatures into the market.
  • If the creature is on a winning streak since your investment, you earn a bonus each time they win more!
